Wonder

British Columbia is known for its breathtaking outdoors, yet most locals have never truly ventured into our backcountry. Wilderness backpacking is a challenging entry activity, as it requires a lot of commitment, preparation, expenses and physical fitness.

Wonder makes backpacking enticing for beginners by teaching locals how to feel confident in our outdoors through guided overnight hikes, gear rentals, and BC specific wilderness and trail education. Wonder is here so we don’t miss out on BC’s hidden beauty.
