  • British Columbia is known for its breathtaking outdoors, yet most locals have never truly adventured into our backcountry. Wilderness backpacking can be challenging to get into, as it requires a lot of commitment, from preparation and expenses to physical fitness. Wonder makes backpacking enticing for beginners by teaching locals how to feel confident in the outdoors through guided hikes, easy rentals, and BC-specific wilderness and trail education. This helps more people fall in love with our backyard and ensures they don’t miss out on BC’s hidden beauty.
