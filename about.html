Welcome to my tiny space on the internet! My name is Sasha (Mei) Lun and I’m a designer specialised in graphic design and branding. I love the idea that design has the power to influence good change in the world. I pursue branding to help others achieve their dreams, share their ideas, and create encounters that resonate both emotionally and aesthetically.

When I’m not comparing colour swatches, you’ll catch me running on a field, cooking noodle soup, standing in thunderstorms, and thinking about the meaning of life...
